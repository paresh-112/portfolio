NG Cater

AI-Powered Catering Marketplace

In Production

A marketplace connecting businesses with caterers — where ordering used to mean phone calls and PDF menus. I built the AI layer that removes that friction on both sides.

  • Conversational ordering: customers describe what they need in plain English — the assistant finds matching caterers, browses menus, and helps assemble orders
  • Menu digitization in minutes: caterers upload a PDF or photo of their menu and it becomes a structured, searchable listing — replacing hours of manual data entry per vendor
  • Faster supply onboarding + natural-language discovery — lowering the barrier for vendors to join and for buyers to find them
  • Agent orchestration: LangGraph multi-step agent on AWS Bedrock (Claude), with tool-calling into search, menus, and order context
  • Semantic search: hybrid keyword + vector retrieval over OpenSearch indices for caterers, menus, and packages
  • Persistent memory: LangGraph PostgreSQL checkpointer so conversations survive across sessions
  • PII-safe streaming: server-sent-event token streaming with personal data filtered before it reaches the client
  • Menu extraction pipeline: AWS Textract OCR + LLM structuring converts raw menu documents into normalized catalog data
  • Infra: containerized services on ECS Fargate, Redis caching, IAM-scoped access

Ctrl Shft

Compliance & Policy Intelligence Engine

In Production

A compliance platform for organizations in regulated care sectors, where policy work, incident handling, and document verification are slow, manual, and high-stakes. I built a suite of AI services that turns those workflows from hours into minutes.

  • Policy intelligence: drafts and rewrites organizational policies aligned to regulatory frameworks
  • Incident & complaint triage: classifies severity, extracts key facts, and suggests next actions consistently
  • Risk & verification: excursion risk assessments, provider document verification, and CV parsing — reducing manual review workload while improving auditability

  • Document AI: classification + verification pipelines for provider-supplied documents
  • Structured outputs: schema-validated LLM responses so downstream systems consume results safely
  • Cost governance: per-request AI usage and cost tracking built into the platform

My Greek Odyssey

AI Travel Companion

In Production

A travel platform for exploring Greece and its islands. Static destination content doesn't answer a traveler's actual questions — so I built an AI companion that does.

  • Interactive concierge: travelers ask about destinations, itineraries, and logistics in natural language and get grounded answers from the platform's curated knowledge
  • Higher engagement: turns a content site into a conversation — keeping users planning inside the platform instead of leaving to search elsewhere
  • Cost-aware by design: built-in usage tracking keeps AI spend predictable as the audience grows
  • Agentic chat service: FastAPI backend with a clean route → controller → service architecture and a dedicated travel agent module
  • Knowledge retrieval: curated destination knowledge base powering grounded, on-brand answers
  • Production hygiene: request tracing with per-request IDs, structured logging, uniform response envelopes, centralized error handling
  • Quality gates: strict typing (mypy --strict), linting, and pre-commit hooks; background workers for async jobs
  • Cross-platform client: Flutter app (clean architecture) consuming the AI APIs
